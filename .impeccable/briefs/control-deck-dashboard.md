# Surface brief — Control Deck console

Mode: Operate
Primary target: src/App.jsx

Audience: a Windows operator monitoring protected client continuity while working.
Job: confirm the fail-closed lane is healthy, rotate the public /64, and read the receipt without leaving the dashboard.
Success: protection, addresses, edge, controls, and the latest rotation are legible within one scan; every control reports into the log.
Constraints: the detailed brief pins the layout, copy, sample data, dark navy visual world, electric blue and green accents, and simulated backend. No real network or OS actions. Product, package, log, and launch labels use neutral client terminology.

Memorable moment: one click sends a visible "hop" through the address rail, the hop count, and a new green rotation receipt in the log.

## Direction contract

THESIS: a live network control deck with the density and calm of professional instrumentation, not a generic card dashboard with neon paint. It refuses the usual SaaS hero-card mush by making the /64 address, protection state, and log receipt the visual spine.

OWN-WORLD: blue-black ground with a faint coordinate grid; graphite/navy instrument panels separated by hairline rules; Archivo for UI and IBM Plex Mono for addresses, timestamps, and readouts; electric blue for system identity and action structure, vivid mint green reserved for protection, success, and the one primary hop action; small machined radii, no glass cards, no gradient text, one restrained green glow around the primary control.

STORY: the operator sees PROTECTED at the top right, the current /64 and edge in the next band, six policies wired beneath, and the live proof trail at right. A hop updates every proof point and writes the receipt in the same eyeful.

FIRST VIEWPORT: 36px desktop title strip; brand + protection status header; four equal action bays; four status cards; then a two-pane cockpit with six service switches at left and the log console at right. Primary action sits in the action row, third bay, filled green.

FORM: operational console pinned by the client brief; composition is the supplied hierarchy, raised by disciplined instrumentation and one linked hop motion. seed: none (unavailable).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
